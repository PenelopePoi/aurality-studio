Release preparation checklist

Record the title, artist names, writers, producer credits, ownership and splits, recording version, explicit-content status, artwork permissions, release date, and distributor requirements. Keep master files and permission records. This worksheet does not deliver a release to music stores or register rights.

Publishing and getting paid

Copyright permission and monetization eligibility are separate questions. Check current rules before publishing and again when applying for monetization. Keep original footage, project files, licenses, and release documents; record notices and appeal deadlines if a platform takes action. Approval and payment are never guaranteed.

Official sources: YouTube monetization policies, Meta Community Standards, DistroKid release help. Platform links are references, not integrations or affiliations. Policy review date: November 25, 2026; consult current official rules for your platform and content.

Modeling, fire performance & social edits

Keep performer/model permissions, location permission where required, music rights, and an original copy. For a booking reel: identify the act, show representative work, add a clear booking contact, and export for the intended destination. Check fire-performance footage against the platform’s current dangerous-activity and advertising policies; artistic intent alone does not determine eligibility.

Learn through a project

  1. Plan: audience, format, equipment, rights.
  2. Create: record or perform an original piece.
  3. Edit: explain three decisions you made.
  4. Verify: check audio, video, captions, and ownership records.
  5. Reflect: identify one skill improved and one next goal.
